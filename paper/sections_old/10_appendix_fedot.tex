\section{FEDOT.LLM, full configuration}
\label{app:fedot}

\begin{sloppypar}
The leaderboard row of FEDOT.LLM is a lightweight run
(Section~\ref{sec:deviations}). As a check we ran FEDOT.LLM on the eight TabReD
tasks in its full configuration: the best-quality preset, tuning on and a
$1200$\,s backend budget, three attempts per task under the same 8 cores,
16\,GB and $3600$\,s ceiling as the other systems. The last row of
Table~\ref{tab:leaderboard} gives the result.
\end{sloppypar}

On three tasks no attempt returned a metric: FEDOT's type detector treats
columns with few distinct values as categorical and turns the table into a
matrix of Python objects, so 1.7\,GB of features take 17\,GB before any model
is fitted. On the other tasks FEDOT spends most of its budget on the initial
pipeline and skips evolutionary search on all but the smallest table.

On the \fedotFullTasks{} tasks with a result the full configuration scores
$\nFedotFullCommon$ against $\nAutoDSCommon$ for AutoDS-Tools and
$\nTerminusCommon$ for Terminus-2. The lead comes from \texttt{ecom-offers} alone, at $\nFedotFullEcom$, above
every published method; without it the three scores are $\nFedotFullNoEcom$,
$\nAutoDSNoEcom$ and $\nTerminusNoEcom$. Over all eight tasks, counting a task without a result as the weakest published method,
FEDOT.LLM scores $\nFedotFull$ against $\nTerminus$ for Terminus-2 and stays
last. Measured from this row the architectural spans are $\spanArchFull$ and
$\spanShipFull$, against $\spanArch$ and $\spanShip$ from the lightweight row.
